❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about working with Lissia Design.

How long does it take to see SEO results? ▼
SEO is a long-term investment. Most clients begin seeing meaningful improvements in 3-6 months, with significant results typically visible at the 6-12 month mark.
What does website maintenance include? ▼
Our maintenance plans include regular software/plugin updates, security monitoring and patches, uptime monitoring (24/7), monthly backups, speed optimization checks, and up to 2 hours of content updates per month.
How do I pay for services? ▼
We accept all major credit/debit cards, ACH bank transfers, and PayPal. Monthly retainer clients are billed on the 1st of each month. Use the "Pay Now" button in the navigation to quickly update payment information or pay an invoice.
Do I own my website and content? ▼
Yes — 100%. Once your project is paid in full, all work product belongs to you. We transfer all access credentials and asset files to you upon project completion. We never hold your website hostage.
What is your content creation process? ▼
We start with a discovery call to understand your voice, audience, and goals. Our team creates a content calendar for your review and approval before anything is published.
How do I get support or request changes? ▼
Use the "Request Support" button on any page to send a message directly to Gina at gina@lissiadesign.com. We aim to respond to all support requests within 1 business day.
What social media platforms do you manage? ▼
We manage Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, TikTok, Pinterest, and Google Business Profile. Most clients choose 2-3 platforms where their audience is most active.
Can I cancel my services at any time? ▼
Monthly retainer services require 30 days written notice to cancel. We don't believe in locking clients into long-term contracts — we'd rather earn your business every month with great results.

Still have questions?

Gina is happy to help! Reach out directly and we'll get back to you within 1 business day.
